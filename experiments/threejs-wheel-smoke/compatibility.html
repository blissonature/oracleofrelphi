Three.js Explorer · compatibility test

Isolated check of original Relphi glyphs, zodiac math, gestures, WebGL 2 and a working SVG fallback. This is NOT the Sky Chart prototype.

Exact geometry & navigation

Focus 0°. Drag the map, click a longitude, or use arrows. The aspect line and zodiac rotate together under the fixed foreground marker, even without Three.js.

Canonical glyph integrity

These glyphs use the real RelphiGlyphRegistry and RelphiGlyphComponent. They are DOM SVGs, not replacement artwork, raster textures, or newly drawn Three.js meshes.

WebGL and Three.js

A successful graphics result requires the Three.js module to load, a WebGL 2 context, a real draw call, and pixels read from the resulting frame. The camera math is separately checked.

Page/layout resilience

The SVG map and canonical glyphs should still load when WebGL 2 is unavailable. Resize this page and try tab/keyboard operation. No existing Sky Chart controllers have been modified.

The test runs in your browser. Results describe this device/browser, not all browsers.
