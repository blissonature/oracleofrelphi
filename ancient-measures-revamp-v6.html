Rope, Line, and Building

This version models the rope as three measured spans: three, four, and five units. It does not spring, coil, or wrap around the stakes.

How it behaves

Each side has its own fixed length. Pull a stake until one adjacent span becomes taut. Push inward and that span settles into one broad curve on the ground. The solver has no inertia, so the rope stops when your hand stops.
