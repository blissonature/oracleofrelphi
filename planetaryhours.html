Multipliers. Default “Sidereal” mode uses the orbital periods in Earth years as weights. Power for an hour = (DayWeight × HourWeight) × (1/ordinal) + ordinal.

Sunrise→Sunrise frame. The day begins at local sunrise and ends at the next sunrise.

Legend. Bright = post-sunrise half; Dark = post-sunset half.
