Transiting Uranus Retrograde Opposite Part of Fortune

A concise symbolic interpretation, with the distinction between a transiting planet and a natal calculated point made explicit.

↔ 180° aspect

Simple definition

Transiting Uranus retrograde opposite the natal Part of Fortune symbolizes a return to unfinished change. Ways of living that once felt natural or sustaining may need a new form, so freedom and fulfillment can meet rather than compete.

What each part of the phrase means

Transiting Uranus is Uranus's moving position in the current sky. Uranus signifies freedom, disruption, awakening, originality, and creative change. In Relphi's symbolic model, it represents the possibility of making a new form.

Retrograde (Rx) means Uranus appears to move backward along the zodiac from Earth's perspective. This apparent motion is astronomical; its interpretation as a period of reconsideration is symbolic. Relphi treats retrograde as reworking—not as an automatic reversal of good fortune.

Opposite means the positions are approximately 180° apart. The opposition sets two principles face to face. It calls for relationship and adjustment, not a simplistic declaration that one must defeat the other.

Part of Fortune, also called Fortuna or Pars Fortunae, is a calculated chart point derived from the Sun, Moon, and Ascendant. In Relphi's vocabulary it represents the meeting place of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease; it does not literally guarantee wealth.

How this combination reads

An arrangement that used to support well-being might no longer fit the person becoming capable of a different life. Uranus presses for invention, while the Part of Fortune points toward a lived sense of fit. At an opposition, that tension becomes noticeable: keeping the old arrangement can constrain change, but pursuing change without regard for what sustains you can also scatter energy.

The retrograde phase revisits the creative problem. Rather than treating disruption as the point, the question becomes: what can be redesigned so that new freedom also remains livable? A practice, relationship to work, daily routine, or assumption about fulfillment may be ready for revision. The chart does not by itself specify which one.

Does Uranus retrograde repeat this opposition?

It can. Because Uranus changes its apparent direction, the same natal degree can be contacted on an initial forward pass, a retrograde return, and a later forward pass. This offers a useful sequence—encounter, reworking, integration—but three exact hits are not guaranteed. The number and dates depend on the actual planetary positions and the selected orb.

Uranus moves slowly, so the larger period can span months or longer. An exact date, pass count, or active interval should be calculated from the chart rather than asserted by a general glossary page.

Which Part of Fortune is being opposed?

Transiting Uranus opposite your natal Part of Fortune compares Uranus in the current sky with the point fixed in a birth chart. That is the interpretation on this page.

Transiting Uranus opposite a transiting Part of Fortune is different: both coordinates belong to a current sky. The calculated Part of Fortune changes with the Sun, Moon, and local Ascendant, so time and location matter and the contact need not describe a months-long personal process.

How to make the reading specific

  1. Check the signs and houses involved; they describe where the contrast is expressed.
  2. Check the orb and whether Uranus is approaching, at, or leaving an exact opposition.
  3. Confirm Uranus's motion and exact-pass history, rather than assuming a standard three-pass cycle.
  4. Verify the birth time and calculation convention for the natal Part of Fortune before drawing conclusions from its degree or house.

Common questions

Does this mean bad luck?

No. It is not a reliable prediction of losses or gains. Symbolically, it asks how change might alter the conditions in which fulfillment is possible.

Is retrograde Uranus weaker?

Retrograde describes apparent direction of motion, not a measured reduction in physical force. Interpretive traditions vary; Relphi emphasizes revisiting and re-creating what has already taken shape.

Does the aspect predict a sudden event?

No event follows automatically from one aspect. Reading the chart requires its particular degrees, houses, scope, and timing—and even then remains an interpretation rather than a factual forecast.
