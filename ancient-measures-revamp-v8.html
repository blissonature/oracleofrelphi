Rope, Line, and Building

This version uses a real physics world: gravity, rigid colliders, friction, damping, and jointed rope segments.

What changed

1
Gravity is real

Rapier pulls every free rope segment down onto the ground.

2
Poles are colliders

Rope particles cannot occupy the same space as a stake.

3
Length is joint-constrained

Each span contains a fixed number of equal links totaling three, four, or five units.

Construction

Drag a red stake slowly. The rope endpoints follow tangent contact points at the outside of each pole. The free links settle under gravity instead of being drawn as a decorative curve.

Physics settings

Rope links use high linear and angular damping, zero bounce, strong ground friction, and spherical joints. The poles and ground are fixed colliders.
