Essential vocabulary

Short definitions

Transit

The current position of a body in the sky, considered in relation to another chart position—often a natal one. The phrase must identify which side is moving.

Retrograde (Rx)

Apparent backward motion against the zodiac as seen from Earth. Interpretively, it can indicate revisiting and reworking a theme; it is not inherently unfavorable.

Opposition

An aspect formed at approximately 180° of zodiacal longitude. Two functions face each other and require a conscious relationship rather than automatic agreement.

Uranus

The planetary referent of freedom, originality, disruption, awakening, and new forms. In Relphi’s symbolic reference, its creative action is to bring a new form into being.

Part of Fortune (Fortuna)

A calculated point made from the Sun, Moon, and Ascendant. Relphi reads it as the meeting place of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease—not a promise of money or luck.

Natal position

The zodiacal position of a planet or calculated point at a person's birth. A transit opposing a natal point differs from two positions opposing one another in the present sky.

Orb

The distance, in degrees, between an exact aspect and the positions being compared. An opposition exactly 180° apart has an orb of 0°.

Exact pass

A moment when a moving position reaches the exact aspect angle. Retrograde cycles can bring repeated passes, but the number must be calculated rather than assumed.

Why we separate definitions from chart readings

A glossary entry explains the symbols without pretending to know the reader's birth chart. Signs, houses, timing, and the selected relationship scope make an actual reading more specific. A natal Part of Fortune also depends on an accurate birth time and a consistently chosen calculation convention.

These descriptions are symbolic interpretations, not predictions of particular events. For individual placements, compare your skies in Sky Chart; for fundamentals, see Astrology Foundations.
