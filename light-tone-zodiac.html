Light-Tone Zodiac

A Relphi instrument for ordering light, hearing the zodiac, and translating position into tone, color, and tarot. The musical and color mappings are deliberate sonifications and swatch translations, not claims that the zodiac literally is a piano.

Polarized Light Classifier

Before color, light can be ordered by orientation. Adjust the components and phase to see the classification.

Zodiac Harmonic Instrument

Run your finger along the circumference to hear a slide. Snap the wheel to signs or decans, then listen to aspect ratios.

Translation Layer

The selected degree is translated three ways: a tone for hearing, a color swatch for seeing, and a tarot bridge for symbolic reading.

Aspect ratios use a Relphi sonification convention: opposition as octave, trine as perfect fifth, square as perfect fourth, sextile as a six-to-five interval. This can be refined later without changing the tool’s purpose.
