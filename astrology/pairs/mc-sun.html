Midheaven and Sun

A reference for Midheaven contacting Sun. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Midheaven contacts Sun, the relationship is between public direction, vocation, and visibility and identity, vitality, and conscious purpose; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Midheaven aspect natal Sun

In a transit, Midheaven describes the moving side: public direction, vocation, and visibility. The natal Sun is the established point being contacted: identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. The question is how a period of public direction and contribution meets a personal pattern of purpose and self-expression. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Midheaven aspect Sun

In a natal chart, Midheaven refers to public direction, vocation, and visibility, while Sun refers to identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between public direction and contribution and purpose and self-expression; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to orient decisions toward an outward role, what allows room for purpose and self-expression? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which public direction and contribution and purpose and self-expression are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
