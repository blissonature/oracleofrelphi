Sun and Venus

A reference for Sun contacting Venus. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Sun contacts Venus, the relationship is between identity, vitality, and conscious purpose and values, affection, pleasure, and relationship; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Sun aspect natal Venus

In a transit, Sun describes the moving side: identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. The natal Venus is the established point being contacted: values, affection, pleasure, and relationship. The question is how a period of purpose and self-expression meets a personal pattern of pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Sun aspect Venus

In a natal chart, Sun refers to identity, vitality, and conscious purpose, while Venus refers to values, affection, pleasure, and relationship. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between purpose and self-expression and pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to act from a visible sense of self, what allows room for pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which purpose and self-expression and pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
