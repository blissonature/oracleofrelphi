Mercury and Ascendant

A reference for Mercury contacting Ascendant. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Mercury contacts Ascendant, the relationship is between thought, language, perception, and exchange and the manner of entering situations and being initially perceived; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Mercury aspect natal Ascendant

In a transit, Mercury describes the moving side: thought, language, perception, and exchange. The natal Ascendant is the established point being contacted: the manner of entering situations and being initially perceived. The question is how a period of understanding and communication meets a personal pattern of self-presentation and beginnings. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Mercury aspect Ascendant

In a natal chart, Mercury refers to thought, language, perception, and exchange, while Ascendant refers to the manner of entering situations and being initially perceived. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between understanding and communication and self-presentation and beginnings; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to name, connect, and communicate what is perceived, what allows room for self-presentation and beginnings? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting Mercury retrograde aspect natal Ascendant

When transiting Mercury is retrograde, its apparent return can revisit the meeting of understanding and communication with self-presentation and beginnings. This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which understanding and communication and self-presentation and beginnings are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
