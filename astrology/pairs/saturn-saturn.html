Natal Saturn aspect Saturn

In a single birth chart, Saturn cannot occupy two independent natal longitudes as the same point. A "Saturn–Saturn" comparison is mainly useful across two charts or when transiting Saturn contacts natal Saturn.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to give commitments boundaries and workable form, what allows room for enduring structure and accountability? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting Saturn retrograde aspect natal Saturn

This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.
