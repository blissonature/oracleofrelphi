Sun and Descendant

A reference for Sun contacting Descendant. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Sun contacts Descendant, the relationship is between identity, vitality, and conscious purpose and partnership, contracts, and encounters with another person; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Sun aspect natal Descendant

In a transit, Sun describes the moving side: identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. The natal Descendant is the established point being contacted: partnership, contracts, and encounters with another person. The question is how a period of purpose and self-expression meets a personal pattern of the terms of partnership and exchange. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Sun aspect Descendant

In a natal chart, Sun refers to identity, vitality, and conscious purpose, while Descendant refers to partnership, contracts, and encounters with another person. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between purpose and self-expression and the terms of partnership and exchange; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to act from a visible sense of self, what allows room for the terms of partnership and exchange? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which purpose and self-expression and the terms of partnership and exchange are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
