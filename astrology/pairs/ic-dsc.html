Imum Coeli and Descendant

A reference for Imum Coeli contacting Descendant. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Imum Coeli contacts Descendant, the relationship is between home, roots, private foundations, and belonging and partnership, contracts, and encounters with another person; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Imum Coeli aspect natal Descendant

In a transit, Imum Coeli describes the moving side: home, roots, private foundations, and belonging. The natal Descendant is the established point being contacted: partnership, contracts, and encounters with another person. The question is how a period of home and inherited belonging meets a personal pattern of the terms of partnership and exchange. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Imum Coeli aspect Descendant

In a natal chart, Imum Coeli refers to home, roots, private foundations, and belonging, while Descendant refers to partnership, contracts, and encounters with another person. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between home and inherited belonging and the terms of partnership and exchange; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to protect the foundations from which life grows, what allows room for the terms of partnership and exchange? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.
