Lilith and Imum Coeli

A reference for Lilith contacting Imum Coeli. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Lilith contacts Imum Coeli, the relationship is between instinctive autonomy, refusal, and uncompromised desire and home, roots, private foundations, and belonging; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Lilith aspect natal Imum Coeli

In a transit, Lilith describes the moving side: instinctive autonomy, refusal, and uncompromised desire. The natal Imum Coeli is the established point being contacted: home, roots, private foundations, and belonging. The question is how a period of uncompromised self-direction meets a personal pattern of home and inherited belonging. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Lilith aspect Imum Coeli

In a natal chart, Lilith refers to instinctive autonomy, refusal, and uncompromised desire, while Imum Coeli refers to home, roots, private foundations, and belonging. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between uncompromised self-direction and home and inherited belonging; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to recognize the right to refuse imposed definitions, what allows room for home and inherited belonging? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting Lilith retrograde aspect natal Imum Coeli

When transiting Lilith is retrograde, its apparent return can revisit the meeting of uncompromised self-direction with home and inherited belonging. This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which uncompromised self-direction and home and inherited belonging are experienced. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.
