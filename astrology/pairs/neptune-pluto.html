Neptune and Pluto

A reference for Neptune contacting Pluto. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Neptune contacts Pluto, the relationship is between imagination, sensitivity, surrender, and ideals and power, depth, elimination, and transformation; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Neptune aspect natal Pluto

In a transit, Neptune describes the moving side: imagination, sensitivity, surrender, and ideals. The natal Pluto is the established point being contacted: power, depth, elimination, and transformation. The question is how a period of inspiration and compassionate release meets a personal pattern of depth and meaningful transformation. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Neptune aspect Pluto

In a natal chart, Neptune refers to imagination, sensitivity, surrender, and ideals, while Pluto refers to power, depth, elimination, and transformation. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between inspiration and compassionate release and depth and meaningful transformation; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to loosen fixed categories and follow an imaginative vision, what allows room for depth and meaningful transformation? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting Neptune retrograde aspect natal Pluto

When transiting Neptune is retrograde, its apparent return can revisit the meeting of inspiration and compassionate release with depth and meaningful transformation. This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which inspiration and compassionate release and depth and meaningful transformation are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
