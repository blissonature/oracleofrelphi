Moon and Venus

A reference for Moon contacting Venus. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Moon contacts Venus, the relationship is between feelings, instinct, memory, and emotional needs and values, affection, pleasure, and relationship; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Moon aspect natal Venus

In a transit, Moon describes the moving side: feelings, instinct, memory, and emotional needs. The natal Venus is the established point being contacted: values, affection, pleasure, and relationship. The question is how a period of emotional safety and belonging meets a personal pattern of pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Moon aspect Venus

In a natal chart, Moon refers to feelings, instinct, memory, and emotional needs, while Venus refers to values, affection, pleasure, and relationship. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between emotional safety and belonging and pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to respond to experience and tend emotional memory, what allows room for pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which emotional safety and belonging and pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
