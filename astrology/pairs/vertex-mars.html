Vertex and Mars

A reference for Vertex contacting Mars. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Vertex contacts Mars, the relationship is between encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing and initiative, desire, assertion, and conflict; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Vertex aspect natal Mars

In a transit, Vertex describes the moving side: encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing. The natal Mars is the established point being contacted: initiative, desire, assertion, and conflict. The question is how a period of consequential meetings and response meets a personal pattern of agency and deliberate action. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Vertex aspect Mars

In a natal chart, Vertex refers to encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing, while Mars refers to initiative, desire, assertion, and conflict. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between consequential meetings and response and agency and deliberate action; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to notice the significance assigned to an encounter, what allows room for agency and deliberate action? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which consequential meetings and response and agency and deliberate action are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
