Venus and Sun

A reference for Venus contacting Sun. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Venus contacts Sun, the relationship is between values, affection, pleasure, and relationship and identity, vitality, and conscious purpose; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Venus aspect natal Sun

In a transit, Venus describes the moving side: values, affection, pleasure, and relationship. The natal Sun is the established point being contacted: identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. The question is how a period of pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values meets a personal pattern of purpose and self-expression. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Venus aspect Sun

In a natal chart, Venus refers to values, affection, pleasure, and relationship, while Sun refers to identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values and purpose and self-expression; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to recognize what is valuable and make room for connection, what allows room for purpose and self-expression? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting Venus retrograde aspect natal Sun

When transiting Venus is retrograde, its apparent return can revisit the meeting of pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values with purpose and self-expression. This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which pleasure, reciprocity, and chosen values and purpose and self-expression are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
