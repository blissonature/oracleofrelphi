Vertex and Part of Fortune

A reference for Vertex contacting Part of Fortune. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Vertex contacts Part of Fortune, the relationship is between encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing and the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Vertex aspect natal Part of Fortune

In a transit, Vertex describes the moving side: encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing. The natal Part of Fortune is the established point being contacted: the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease. The question is how a period of consequential meetings and response meets a personal pattern of embodied fit and fulfillment. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Vertex aspect Part of Fortune

In a natal chart, Vertex refers to encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing, while Part of Fortune refers to the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between consequential meetings and response and embodied fit and fulfillment; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to notice the significance assigned to an encounter, what allows room for embodied fit and fulfillment? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which consequential meetings and response and embodied fit and fulfillment are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
