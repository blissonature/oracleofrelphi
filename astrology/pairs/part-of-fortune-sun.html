Part of Fortune and Sun

A reference for Part of Fortune contacting Sun. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Part of Fortune contacts Sun, the relationship is between the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease and identity, vitality, and conscious purpose; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Part of Fortune aspect natal Sun

In a transit, Part of Fortune describes the moving side: the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease. The natal Sun is the established point being contacted: identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. The question is how a period of embodied fit and fulfillment meets a personal pattern of purpose and self-expression. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Part of Fortune aspect Sun

In a natal chart, Part of Fortune refers to the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease, while Sun refers to identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between embodied fit and fulfillment and purpose and self-expression; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to notice where life can be lived with less resistance, what allows room for purpose and self-expression? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which embodied fit and fulfillment and purpose and self-expression are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
