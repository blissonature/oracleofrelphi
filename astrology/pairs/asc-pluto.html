Ascendant and Pluto

A reference for Ascendant contacting Pluto. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Ascendant contacts Pluto, the relationship is between the manner of entering situations and being initially perceived and power, depth, elimination, and transformation; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Ascendant aspect natal Pluto

In a transit, Ascendant describes the moving side: the manner of entering situations and being initially perceived. The natal Pluto is the established point being contacted: power, depth, elimination, and transformation. The question is how a period of self-presentation and beginnings meets a personal pattern of depth and meaningful transformation. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Ascendant aspect Pluto

In a natal chart, Ascendant refers to the manner of entering situations and being initially perceived, while Pluto refers to power, depth, elimination, and transformation. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between self-presentation and beginnings and depth and meaningful transformation; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to meet the world through an embodied point of view, what allows room for depth and meaningful transformation? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which self-presentation and beginnings and depth and meaningful transformation are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
