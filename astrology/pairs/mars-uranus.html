Mars and Uranus

A reference for Mars contacting Uranus. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Mars contacts Uranus, the relationship is between initiative, desire, assertion, and conflict and freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Mars aspect natal Uranus

In a transit, Mars describes the moving side: initiative, desire, assertion, and conflict. The natal Uranus is the established point being contacted: freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention. The question is how a period of agency and deliberate action meets a personal pattern of liberty and inventive change. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Mars aspect Uranus

In a natal chart, Mars refers to initiative, desire, assertion, and conflict, while Uranus refers to freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between agency and deliberate action and liberty and inventive change; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to mobilize effort and assert a direction, what allows room for liberty and inventive change? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting Mars retrograde aspect natal Uranus

When transiting Mars is retrograde, its apparent return can revisit the meeting of agency and deliberate action with liberty and inventive change. This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which agency and deliberate action and liberty and inventive change are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
