Sun and South Node

A reference for Sun contacting South Node. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Sun contacts South Node, the relationship is between identity, vitality, and conscious purpose and familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Sun aspect natal South Node

In a transit, Sun describes the moving side: identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. The natal South Node is the established point being contacted: familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path. The question is how a period of purpose and self-expression meets a personal pattern of the habitual and inherited path. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Sun aspect South Node

In a natal chart, Sun refers to identity, vitality, and conscious purpose, while South Node refers to familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between purpose and self-expression and the habitual and inherited path; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to act from a visible sense of self, what allows room for the habitual and inherited path? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which purpose and self-expression and the habitual and inherited path are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
