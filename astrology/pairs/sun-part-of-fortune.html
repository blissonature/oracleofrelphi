Sun and Part of Fortune

A reference for Sun contacting Part of Fortune. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Sun contacts Part of Fortune, the relationship is between identity, vitality, and conscious purpose and the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Sun aspect natal Part of Fortune

In a transit, Sun describes the moving side: identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. The natal Part of Fortune is the established point being contacted: the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease. The question is how a period of purpose and self-expression meets a personal pattern of embodied fit and fulfillment. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Sun aspect Part of Fortune

In a natal chart, Sun refers to identity, vitality, and conscious purpose, while Part of Fortune refers to the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between purpose and self-expression and embodied fit and fulfillment; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to act from a visible sense of self, what allows room for embodied fit and fulfillment? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which purpose and self-expression and embodied fit and fulfillment are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
