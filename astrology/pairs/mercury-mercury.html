In one sentence

When Mercury contacts Mercury, the relationship is between thought, language, perception, and exchange and thought, language, perception, and exchange; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Mercury aspect natal Mercury

In a transit, Mercury describes the moving side: thought, language, perception, and exchange. The natal Mercury is the established point being contacted: thought, language, perception, and exchange. The question is how a period of understanding and communication meets a personal pattern of understanding and communication. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Mercury aspect Mercury

In a single birth chart, Mercury cannot occupy two independent natal longitudes as the same point. A "Mercury–Mercury" comparison is mainly useful across two charts or when transiting Mercury contacts natal Mercury.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to name, connect, and communicate what is perceived, what allows room for understanding and communication? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting Mercury retrograde aspect natal Mercury

Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.
