Uranus and Sun

A reference for Uranus contacting Sun. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Uranus contacts Sun, the relationship is between freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention and identity, vitality, and conscious purpose; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Uranus aspect natal Sun

In a transit, Uranus describes the moving side: freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention. The natal Sun is the established point being contacted: identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. The question is how a period of liberty and inventive change meets a personal pattern of purpose and self-expression. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Uranus aspect Sun

In a natal chart, Uranus refers to freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention, while Sun refers to identity, vitality, and conscious purpose. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between liberty and inventive change and purpose and self-expression; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to interrupt an old pattern and experiment with a new form, what allows room for purpose and self-expression? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting Uranus retrograde aspect natal Sun

When transiting Uranus is retrograde, its apparent return can revisit the meeting of liberty and inventive change with purpose and self-expression. This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which liberty and inventive change and purpose and self-expression are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
