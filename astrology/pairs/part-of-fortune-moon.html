Part of Fortune and Moon

A reference for Part of Fortune contacting Moon. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Part of Fortune contacts Moon, the relationship is between the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease and feelings, instinct, memory, and emotional needs; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Part of Fortune aspect natal Moon

In a transit, Part of Fortune describes the moving side: the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease. The natal Moon is the established point being contacted: feelings, instinct, memory, and emotional needs. The question is how a period of embodied fit and fulfillment meets a personal pattern of emotional safety and belonging. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Part of Fortune aspect Moon

In a natal chart, Part of Fortune refers to the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease, while Moon refers to feelings, instinct, memory, and emotional needs. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between embodied fit and fulfillment and emotional safety and belonging; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to notice where life can be lived with less resistance, what allows room for emotional safety and belonging? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which embodied fit and fulfillment and emotional safety and belonging are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
