North Node and South Node

A reference for North Node contacting South Node. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When North Node contacts South Node, the relationship is between development through unfamiliar experience and emerging capacity and familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting North Node aspect natal South Node

In a transit, North Node describes the moving side: development through unfamiliar experience and emerging capacity. The natal South Node is the established point being contacted: familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path. The question is how a period of developing capacity and unfamiliar growth meets a personal pattern of the habitual and inherited path. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal North Node aspect South Node

In a natal chart, North Node refers to development through unfamiliar experience and emerging capacity, while South Node refers to familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between developing capacity and unfamiliar growth and the habitual and inherited path; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to move toward skills not yet habitual, what allows room for the habitual and inherited path? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Axis or point note: These positions can describe opposite ends of the same chart axis. In the same sky, a defining opposition is not an additional independent discovery.

Transiting North Node retrograde aspect natal South Node

When transiting North Node is retrograde, its apparent return can revisit the meeting of developing capacity and unfamiliar growth with the habitual and inherited path. This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which developing capacity and unfamiliar growth and the habitual and inherited path are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
