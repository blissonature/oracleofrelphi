Part of Fortune and Uranus

A reference for Part of Fortune contacting Uranus. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When Part of Fortune contacts Uranus, the relationship is between the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease and freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Part of Fortune aspect natal Uranus

In a transit, Part of Fortune describes the moving side: the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease. The natal Uranus is the established point being contacted: freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention. The question is how a period of embodied fit and fulfillment meets a personal pattern of liberty and inventive change. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Part of Fortune aspect Uranus

In a natal chart, Part of Fortune refers to the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease, while Uranus refers to freedom, originality, disruption, and reinvention. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between embodied fit and fulfillment and liberty and inventive change; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to notice where life can be lived with less resistance, what allows room for liberty and inventive change? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which embodied fit and fulfillment and liberty and inventive change are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
