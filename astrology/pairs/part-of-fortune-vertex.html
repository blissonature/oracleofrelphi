In one sentence

When Part of Fortune contacts Vertex, the relationship is between the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease and encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting Part of Fortune aspect natal Vertex

In a transit, Part of Fortune describes the moving side: the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease. The natal Vertex is the established point being contacted: encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing. The question is how a period of embodied fit and fulfillment meets a personal pattern of consequential meetings and response. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal Part of Fortune aspect Vertex

In a natal chart, Part of Fortune refers to the meeting of body, feeling, circumstance, and ease, while Vertex refers to encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between embodied fit and fulfillment and consequential meetings and response; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to notice where life can be lived with less resistance, what allows room for consequential meetings and response? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which embodied fit and fulfillment and consequential meetings and response are experienced. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.
