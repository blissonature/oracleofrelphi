South Node and Vertex

A reference for South Node contacting Vertex. Choose an aspect and refine by houses, signs, and motion on this same page.

In one sentence

When South Node contacts Vertex, the relationship is between familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path and encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing; the aspect determines how those two functions meet.

Transiting South Node aspect natal Vertex

In a transit, South Node describes the moving side: familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path. The natal Vertex is the established point being contacted: encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing. The question is how a period of the habitual and inherited path meets a personal pattern of consequential meetings and response. A transit names a contact between chart positions, not an event that must happen.

Natal South Node aspect Vertex

In a natal chart, South Node refers to familiar patterns, inherited skills, and the known path, while Vertex refers to encounters experienced as consequential or unexpectedly revealing. An actual aspect, if present, describes a lasting relationship between the habitual and inherited path and consequential meetings and response; without exact birth positions, there is no basis for claiming that the two are in aspect.

How to interpret the pair

A useful reading question is: when someone needs to draw upon experience while recognizing its limits, what allows room for consequential meetings and response? The answer depends on the exact aspect, the signs, the houses and the chart context.

Transiting South Node retrograde aspect natal Vertex

When transiting South Node is retrograde, its apparent return can revisit the meeting of the habitual and inherited path with consequential meetings and response. This does not make the aspect adverse. Repeated exact passes are possible but require actual longitude and date calculations; a glossary cannot assume there are three.

What information makes a reading specific?

Houses identify the life areas through which the habitual and inherited path and consequential meetings and response are experienced. Signs describe the mode of expression; degrees establish whether an exact aspect exists. For a transit, the moving body's date and retrograde status also matter. A birth-time-dependent point requires reliable time and location data.

Relphi's interpretations are symbolic rather than forecasts of guaranteed events. No house choice or sign selection proves that the aspect is present.
