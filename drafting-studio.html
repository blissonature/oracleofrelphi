Four handles. Four distinct actions.

Red · Right caliper point: move the whole instrument; no line is drawn. Gray · Left caliper point: change orientation without marking the paper.

Gold · Pencil holder: slide outward or inward to increase or decrease the diameter (twice the radius from the right point). Blue · Pencil tip: rotate around the right point and draw. With spiral mode on, drag the tip inward or outward as you rotate; or use Trace 360° for an exact spiral with the specified growth.

Optional snaps: choose grid intersections (25 units), existing geometry and line crossings, 15° rotation, or radius increments (5 units). They are independent, start off, and never change previously drawn marks.

Works with mouse, pen, or touch. Two fingers can operate the holder and pencil together. Circle and polygon buttons create mathematically exact vector constructions. SVG and PNG exports contain the marks, not the hardware or guides.
